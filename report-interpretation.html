📋 Full Interpretation Report

This page reads every report from every department and explains it in simple words: what is going well, what is not, what needs fixing right now, how departments compare, and what to do next. It updates by itself every time a new report comes in.

✅ 1. Strengths — What Each Department Is Doing Well

Activities at 90% or more of their target, taken from each department's most recent report.

⚠️ 2. Weaknesses — What Needs Improvement

Activities between 60% and 89% of their target. Not failing, but not yet on track.

🚨 3. Critical Issues — Fix Immediately

Activities below 60% of their target. The department at the top of this list needs help first.

📊 4. Compare All Departments

Every department's latest overall score, ranked from highest to lowest. 🟢 90%+ on target · 🟠 60–89% at risk · 🔴 below 60% critical.

💡 5. Recommendations

Simple next steps, generated automatically from the numbers above.

🗓️ 6. Monthly Reports

Every report a department has ever sent, one chip per month, in order. This is the history the quarterly reports below are built from.

📦 7. Quarterly Reports (Auto-Built From Every 4 Monthly Reports)

As soon as a department has sent 4 monthly reports, the system adds their numbers together and creates the next quarterly report by itself. No one has to do this by hand.
